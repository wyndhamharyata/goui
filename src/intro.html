goui

Browser level EQ to show how easy and significant it is to adjust EQ for your headphone against your own ear, because of a very simple mantra: different ear, different head, different sound.

How it works

Tab audio → HeadphoneAutoEQ → My earEQByEar → Your tasteBass, Vocals, Treble → You

This extension works on the notion of joining two incredibly important audio tools: AutoEQ and EQByEar. AutoEQ will give you a good neutral baseline against the headphone you personally use. Then use EQByEar to adjust the fine details that are specific to the interaction between your headphone / IEM and your hearing. Add flavour on top of that, based on your taste.

Make it yours

  1. HD 600 Sennheiser HD 600AutoEQ Save

    Find your headphoneSearch your model in the Library and hit Save. That's your neutral baseline.

  2. Measure my ear→Paste→Save

    Tune it to your earTake the EQByEar test, paste the result into My ear, and save it. Optional, but this is where it gets personal.

  3. Add your flavourDrag the points in Tune, or nudge Bass, Vocals and Treble in Listen. Go with what sounds good to you.
