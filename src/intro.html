goui

goui runs the sound of one browser tab through an EQ. It tunes the sound to your headphone, then to your ear, then to your taste. Different ear, different head, different sound.

YouHear it

goui joins two audio tools. AutoEQ gives you a neutral baseline for the headphone you use. An EQByEar test tunes that baseline to your hearing. Your taste goes on top, as bands of your own. Each layer has its own switch. The bands of every layer add up in dB.

    Sets

    • A Set names one headphone preset, one ear preset and one taste preset, with Bass, Vocals and Treble.
    • Pick a Set in the picker at the top of Listen, or in the Sets tab of the Library.
    • Save as Set needs each layer with bands saved as a preset first. If a layer is not saved, goui names it and stops.
    • To change a Set, save under its name. The button reads Replace.
    • The × on a row in the Sets tab deletes that Set.
    • An empty ear or taste slot turns that layer off. An empty headphone slot plays Flat.
    • A Set points at its presets by name. An edit to a preset reaches every Set that names it.
    • A deleted preset reads as missing in the Set. It plays as an empty slot.
    • Macgoui for the Mac lists your Sets in the menu bar item's right-click menu. goui set "name" plays one from Terminal.

    The icon is the switch

    Chrome lets an extension hear a tab only after a click on its icon. The panel has no Start button.

    • With the panel open, click the icon on the tab goui hears to turn goui off. With the panel closed, the same click only opens the panel.
    • Click the icon on another tab to move goui there.
    • The icon shows a green ON on the tab goui hears.
    • Closing the tab goui hears turns goui off.
    • PopupAn installed app, such as YT Music, has no side panel. There the icon opens goui as a popup, with its own power button.
    • MacThe extension changes the sound of one browser tab. For every sound on the Mac, use goui for the Mac.

    Measure your ear

    An AutoEQ profile tunes the headphone to sound neutral, not your ear. EQByEar measures what your ear still wants on top of it.

    1 Turn My ear offIn the Library, open My ear and press Measure my ear with EQByEar. My ear turns off so the test hears the headphone alone.
    2 Run the testEQByEar opens in a new tab. Click the goui icon on that tab first so the test hears your headphone layer. Then run the test. With goui for the Mac, goui already hears the browser. Run the test right away.
    3 Paste the resultCopy the result and come back to goui. Press Paste in My ear, paste it in the box, then press Apply.
    4 Save itPress Save to keep it as an ear preset. Turn My ear on to hear the headphone with your ear.
