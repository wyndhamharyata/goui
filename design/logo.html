goui design · logo proposal

One mark.
Six ways to draw it.

The icon that ships is a blue to purple tile with four white bars. It is the one blue left in goui. This page tries six marks in the acid accent and the ink steps. Each one is shown at the four extension sizes, on a light toolbar and a dark one, with a pixel view of the 16 px icon as Chrome would draw it. Each one is also drawn in one colour for the Mac menu bar, and placed in the menu bar, the store tile, the site header, the Dock and a browser tab. Section 05 picks one and lists what has to change to ship it.

01

Six concepts

Each one comes from a different idea, not from a different detail. Four take the ink tile with an acid mark, the way the panel reads. Two turn that round, an acid tile with an ink mark, so a light toolbar and a dark one both show a clear edge. Beside each is its one colour form on a light bar and a dark bar.

02

At the real sizes

Chrome draws the toolbar icon at 16 px and the store shows it at 128 px with 16 px clear on every side. The script keeps that same clear band in every size, so the tile in the 16 px icon is 12 px wide. The strips below draw each icon that way, as a vector. The pixel view on the right rasterises the same SVG at 16 px and 32 px and blows it up, so it shows what a screen at 1x would show. A mark that only reads at 128 is not a logo.

The pixel view is drawn by this page into a canvas at the real size, then scaled up with no smoothing. It is close to what the icon script's own sampler would write, which takes 16 samples per pixel.

03

One colour, for the menu bar

The Mac menu bar icon is a template image. It is drawn in one colour at 18 points and the menu bar paints it black or white to match itself. Each mark below is its own mono symbol, not the colour one with the tile removed. The strokes are thicker and the tile is gone, since the bar gives the mark no frame. The open state shows the item pressed, as it is while its menu is open.

In context

The Chrome Web Store small tile at 440 by 280 px, the site header, the Mac Dock on Apple's grid and a browser tab with the favicon. 05

The recommendation

Concept E, the letter g on an acid tile. Below it is the reasoning, then the list of what has to change to ship it.

E. The letter g, ink on acid

  • It reads at 16 px, and it is the only one that does without doubt. In the pixel view the ring, the stem and the tail are three clear things at 12 px of tile. The bars hold up too, but as four grey sticks. The bell of B reads as a hill. The ear and the waveform go to a blob.
  • It is the loudest thing in the toolbar. An acid tile with an ink letter has a clear edge on the light toolbar and the dark one. Every ink tile on this page is near the dark toolbar's grey, and the acid mark alone has to carry it there.
  • One mark, everywhere. The same g is the favicon, the Dock icon, the menu bar glyph and the mark beside the wordmark. In the site header the g next to the word goui reads as one thing, where a curve or bars next to the word read as two.
  • The mono form is a letter, which the menu bar is used to. The bars of A read fine there. The bell of B reads as a tent. The strips of C read as a menu glyph and the pills of D as a microphone.
  • What it gives up. A letter says nothing about sound. The tagline, the plot and the panel say that instead. The g here is drawn in the spirit of Atkinson Hyperlegible, not from its outline, so the wordmark and the mark are cousins, not twins.
  • Runners up. A is the cheapest change, since only the colours move, and it says equaliser at once. If the owner wants a picture of sound over a letter, A is the pick. B is the pick if the icon should be the plot, and its 16 px reading is close behind A.
  • Calls made on this page. The g is single storey, with a round bowl and a tail that hooks left under it. The stroke is 9 of 64 units, so it is 1.7 px at 12 px of tile. The tile is the accent and the letter is accent-ink, so the icon reads as a pressed accent button, which is how the panel's primary button looks.

What shipping E would touch

All of it is in the two icon scripts and the files they write. Nothing in src/ changes.

WhereTodayFor concept E
scripts/icon-geometry.mjs tileColors is two blues. layOutIcon returns a tile and four bar rectangles from barHeights. One tile colour, #d4ff3f, and one mark colour, #0b0c0e. layOutIcon returns the tile and the g as three strokes on the 64 unit grid scaled to the artwork: a ring with a centre, a radius and a width, a straight stem, and the tail as one cubic curve. The README and store pictures read the same layout, so they follow.
scripts/make-icons.mjs sampleTileColor tests each pixel against the rounded rectangles. layOutMenuBarBars scales the bars alone to 85 percent of the menu bar size. Three tests take the place of the bar test. A pixel is on the ring when its distance from the centre is within half the stroke of the radius. It is on the stem when the existing rounded rectangle test says so. It is on the tail when its distance to the cubic is under half the stroke, which the code can get by flattening the cubic into about 24 line segments and taking the nearest one. The menu bar function lays out the mono g, thicker, with no tile.
icons/icon16.png to icon128.png Blue tile, white bars, 16 px clear at 128. Written again by npm run icons. Same sizes, same clear band.
mac/icons/AppIcon.iconset Ten PNGs from 16 to 1024 px on the 824 of 1024 grid. Written again by the same script. A second pass through iconutil makes the .icns the app bundles. The Dock row above shows the acid tile against a mid blue wallpaper. It is the brightest icon in the Dock, which is a call the owner should make with their own Dock open.
mac/icons/menu-bar.png, @2x The four bars in black, alpha only. The mono g in black, alpha only, at 18 and 36 px. The strokes in mono-e are 10 units of 64, which is 2.8 px at 18 px, and the bowl's hole is 17 units, which is 4.8 px. Both read in the pixel view.

06

Palette note

Every mark on this page uses the accent and the ink steps, and nothing else. The current icon is the one blue left in goui. Once it goes, the sky blue of a shelf band and the lavender of the headphone layer are the only blues, and both are audio colours, not brand colours. No concept here brings a new blue in.

accent#d4ff3f, the mark
ink-1#0e1013, the tile
accent-ink#0b0c0e, the mark on an acid tile
ink-4#282d36, the midline in B
text-1#f2f3f5, the point in B
  • Two polarities. An ink tile with an acid mark matches the panel. An acid tile with an ink mark reads on any toolbar and is the loudest thing in it. The recommendation takes the acid tile. If the owner wants the quieter one, E inverts cleanly, as an ink tile with an acid g, and the Dock row shows how the ink tiles of A, B, D and F look there.
  • No gradient. The current tile fades top to bottom. A flat tile matches the token set, which is flat ink in five steps, and a flat tile is one colour test in the icon script instead of a mix.
  • The menu bar takes no colour. Only alpha counts there. The mono symbols are drawn in currentColor so this page can show them black and white, and the script writes black.
