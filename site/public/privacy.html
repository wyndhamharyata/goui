Privacy

goui collects no personal data. No account, no analytics, no ads. Nothing leaves your browser or Mac except the AutoEQ downloads described below.

Last updated 15 October 2026. This policy covers the goui extension, version 0.1.3 and later. goui for the Mac follows the same rules. The privacy note on the Mac page covers what differs.

What goui reads

  • The active tab. goui reads its address (to check it is a web page) and its title (to show in the panel). It reads no other tab.
  • The tab's audio. The sound runs through the EQ in your browser and plays back. goui never records, stores or sends it.

What goui keeps

Everything stays in your browser's extension storage, on your device.

  • EQ settings and slider values.
  • Headphone, ear and taste presets.
  • Sets, under the sets key. Each Set holds three preset names and slider values.
  • The last tab and graph view used. Whether goui has heard a tab yet.
  • A copy of the AutoEQ headphone list, cached for a week.
  • The title of the tab goui hears, until the browser closes.

Removing the extension deletes all of it.

What goui sends

goui makes one kind of request. When you search AutoEQ or pick a headphone, it downloads the headphone list and profile from GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com). GitHub sees your IP address and the file name. The GitHub privacy statement covers those requests. goui sends no settings, presets, Sets, titles or audio.

The Measure my ear with EQByEar button opens eqbyear.com in a new tab. That visit falls under EQByEar's own policy. goui only reads the result you paste back.

goui for the Mac

The Mac app reads all audio output instead of one tab. It keeps settings, presets and Sets in ~/Library/Application Support/goui. It checks GitHub for new releases twice a day. That request carries nothing about you. Details on the Mac page.

What goui shares

goui collects no data, so it has none to share or sell.

This website

goui.mwyndham.dev runs on Cloudflare. No cookies, no analytics. Cloudflare handles each request under its own privacy policy.
