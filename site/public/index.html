goui for the Mac

The same EQ, for every sound on the Mac. Not one tab, the whole output.

goui lives in the menu bar. One click opens the same panel as the browser extension. It covers Music, Spotify, calls and games at once.

The headphone, ear and taste layers carry over, with Bass, Vocals and Treble. So do the AutoEQ search and the live spectrum. A native audio engine runs the EQ on the Mac. goui keeps none of the audio.

Sets carry over too. Right-click the menu bar item to switch Sets without opening the panel. goui set "name" does the same from Terminal.

It starts at login and restarts after a crash. If the sound stops, it rebuilds the audio path.

Wide panel

Widen the panel, and Listen, Tune and Library stand side by side.

Install

One line in Terminal. No admin password. It checks the Mac and asks before installing.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wyndhamharyata/goui/main/mac/install.sh | sh

Needs Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. goui is not signed by Apple yet. An engine update asks to record audio again. Read more before installing, including what happens to Spatial Audio on AirPods.

How it works

Three layers. One for the headphone, one for your ear, one for your taste.

Every headphone colours the sound. AutoEQ measures that colour and writes a correction profile. That is the headphone layer.

Your hearing is your own. No profile can know it. An EQByEar test measures what your ear still wants on top of the corrected sound. That is the ear layer.

Taste is the third layer, with bands of your own. Bass, Vocals and Treble adjust the sound on top of all three layers. Each layer has its own switch.

The layers add up in dB. The preamp follows the largest boost to prevent clipping.

A Set names one preset per layer, with the sliders. Pick it on Listen and the whole sound loads at once. Editing a preset updates every Set that uses it.

Install

One click from the Chrome Web Store. Chrome keeps it up to date.

Or load the folder by hand

Four steps, one time. No build, no account.

  1. Download the zip. Open the Releases page and press the newest zip. Unzip it.
  2. Load the folder. Open chrome://extensions, or brave://extensions. Turn on Developer mode. Press Load unpacked and pick the goui folder.
  3. Pin goui. Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin goui.
  4. Click the icon. Open a page with sound and click the goui icon on it.

The README has a picture for each step and explains the developer mode warning.
