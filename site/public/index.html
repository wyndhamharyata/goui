goui

Different ear, different head, different sound.

Your headphone, tuned to your ears and your taste. AutoEQ makes it neutral, an EQByEar test fits it to your hearing, and your own bands go on top, for any tab or your whole Mac.

goui for the Mac

The same EQ, for every sound the Mac plays. Not one tab, the whole Mac.

goui lives in the menu bar. One click opens the panel you know from the browser, and it EQs Music, Spotify, a call or a game the same way, all at once.

The headphone, ear and taste layers and Bass, Vocals and Treble all carry over, with the AutoEQ search and the live spectrum. The sound runs through the EQ on your Mac, in a native audio engine, and goui keeps none of it.

It starts at login, comes back on its own after a crash, and builds its audio path again if the sound stops.

Wide panel

Widen it, and Listen, Tune and Library stand side by side, as in a wide Chrome side panel.

Install

One line in Terminal. No admin password. It checks your Mac and asks before it installs.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wyndhamharyata/goui/main/mac/install.sh | sh

Needs a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 26 or newer. goui is not signed by Apple yet, so each update asks to record the audio again. Read more before you install, including what happens to Spatial Audio on AirPods.

How it works

Three layers, kept apart. One for the headphone, one for your ear, one for your taste.

A headphone has its own colour. AutoEQ measures it and writes a profile that cancels it, so the headphone sounds neutral. That is the headphone layer.

Your hearing is your own too, and no profile can know it. An EQByEar test measures what your ear still wants on top of the neutral sound. That is the ear layer.

Your taste is the third layer: bands of your own, for the sound you like on any headphone. Bass, Vocals and Treble go on top. The layers add up in dB, so the merge is exact, and the preamp follows the largest boost, so the sound never clips. Each layer has its own switch, so you can hear AutoEQ alone in one press.

Install

One click from the Chrome Web Store, and Chrome keeps goui up to date.

Or load the folder by hand

Four steps, one time. No build, no account.

  1. Download the zip. Open the Releases page and press the newest zip. Unzip it.
  2. Load the folder. Open chrome://extensions, or brave://extensions, turn on Developer mode, then press Load unpacked and pick the goui folder.
  3. Pin goui. Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin goui.
  4. Click the icon. Open a page with sound and click the goui icon on it.

The README has a picture for each step, and a note about the Chrome warning on developer mode extensions.
