goui for the Mac

The same parametric EQ for all system audio on your Mac.

goui lives in your menu bar, sharing the exact same panel as the browser extension. A single system-wide EQ processes Apple Music, Spotify, calls, and games all at once. Layer adjustments for your headphones, your ear profile, and your taste, with dedicated Bass, Vocals, and Treble sliders.

Search AutoEQ profiles and watch the live spectrum. Powered by a native C and Swift engine, goui processes audio in real time with zero data stored. Right-click the icon to switch Sets instantly, and seamlessly adapts whenever you switch output devices.

Wide panel

Widen the panel to view Listen, Tune and Library side by side.

Install

One command in Terminal. No admin password required.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wyndhamharyata/goui/main/mac/install.sh | sh

Requires Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later. goui is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet, so engine updates prompt for audio permission again. Read the Mac guide before installing, including notes on AirPods Spatial Audio.

How it works

Three separate layers for your headphone, your hearing and your taste.

Tab audio Any tab, or the whole Mac

The extension EQs one browser tab. goui for the Mac EQs all system audio.

Headphone AutoEQ baseline

Every headphone colours the sound. AutoEQ measures that frequency response and builds a correction profile to make it neutral.

My ear EQByEar test

Your ears have their own response on top of the headphone. An EQByEar test measures what your hearing still needs.

Taste Your own bands

Taste holds your own custom bands. Toggle any of the three layers independently.

You Headphones on

All active bands sum in dB. The auto preamp offsets the highest boost to prevent clipping.

Bass, Vocals and Treble

Three sliders nudge the sound on top of all three layers. They stay put when you switch presets.

Set

A Set links a headphone preset, ear preset, taste preset and slider values. Updating a preset updates every Set that references it.

Hover or tap a step to read what it does.

Install

Install from the Chrome Web Store for automatic updates.

Or load unpacked

Four steps. No build step or account required.

  1. Download the zip. Get the latest zip from the Releases page and unzip it.
  2. Load the folder. Open chrome://extensions or brave://extensions, enable Developer mode, click Load unpacked, and select the unzipped goui folder.
  3. Pin goui. Click the puzzle piece in the toolbar and pin goui.
  4. Click the icon. Open a tab playing audio and click the goui icon.

See the install guide for step-by-step screenshots.
