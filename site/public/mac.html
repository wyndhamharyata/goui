goui for the Mac

goui runs every sound the Mac plays through an EQ, from the menu bar. It tunes the sound to your headphone, then to your ear, then to your taste. Every ear hears differently.

What it is

goui for the Mac is the same EQ as the browser extension, for every sound on the Mac. The extension changes one browser tab. The Mac app changes the whole output, so Music, Spotify, calls and games all pass through it at once.

Music · Zoom · SafariEvery sound the Mac plays
1 · HeadphoneAutoEQ tunes the headphone to sound neutral
2 · My earEQByEar tunes it to your hearing
3 · TasteYour own bands
Added after 3 sound layersBass, Vocals and Treble
PreampKeeps the sum from clipping
YouHear it

goui lives in the menu bar. One click opens the same panel as the browser, with Listen, Tune and Library. The headphone, ear and taste layers carry over, with Bass, Vocals and Treble. So do the AutoEQ search, the EQByEar test and the live spectrum. A native audio engine runs the EQ on the Mac. goui keeps none of the audio.

goui joins two audio tools. AutoEQ gives you a neutral baseline for the headphone you use. An EQByEar test tunes that baseline to your hearing. Your taste goes on top, as bands of your own. Each layer has its own switch. The bands of every layer add up in dB. The preamp works itself out from the sum, so a boost does not clip.

Before you install

Install with one line in Terminal. No admin password. It checks the Mac and asks before installing.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wyndhamharyata/goui/main/mac/install.sh | sh
  • goui needs Apple silicon and macOS 26 or later.
  • goui EQs every sound on the Mac. It follows the default output. One EQ covers every app.
  • Another system EQ, such as eqMac or SoundSource, stacks its curve on goui's. Run one at a time.
  • Spatial Audio and head tracking on AirPods and Beats stop while goui plays. Troubleshooting says why.
  • macOS asks to let goui record audio. goui EQs the sound on its way out and keeps none of it.
  • goui is not signed by Apple yet. An engine update asks for that permission again.
  • Settings stay in ~/Library/Application Support/goui, through updates and after an uninstall.

Sets

  • A Set names one headphone preset, one ear preset and one taste preset, with Bass, Vocals and Treble.
  • Pick a Set in the picker at the top of Listen, or in the Sets tab of the Library.
  • Save as Set needs each layer with bands saved as a preset first. If a layer is not saved, goui names it and stops.
  • To change a Set, save under its name. The button reads Replace.
  • The × on a row in the Sets tab deletes that Set.
  • An empty ear or taste slot turns that layer off. An empty headphone slot plays Flat.
  • A Set points at its presets by name. An edit to a preset reaches every Set that names it.
  • A deleted preset reads as missing in the Set. It plays as an empty slot.
  • MenuRight-click the menu bar item. The Sets submenu lists your Sets and ticks the one that plays. Pick one to play it with the panel closed.
  • Terminalgoui set "name" plays a Set from Terminal, a shortcut or a login item. It is the same as a pick from the menu.
  • A Set with more than 32 bands on, counting the three sliders, does not play. The Sets tab says why.

The menu bar item

A click on the menu bar item opens and closes the panel. A right-click opens the menu.

About gouiThe version of goui, and the version the panel runs
Restart engineRebuilds the audio path. The sound plays briefly without the EQ
Reload panelLoads the panel again. The sound carries on
Wide panelWidens the panel so Listen, Tune and Library stand side by side. A second pick takes it back
SetsYour saved Sets, with a tick on the one that plays. With no Sets, it says where to save one
Check for updates…Asks GitHub for a newer release and says what it found
Update to …Shows when a release changes the engine. One click installs it and restarts goui
Automatic updatesA tick means goui checks twice a day. On by default
Quit gouiStops goui until the next login. The Mac plays the normal way again

goui updates itself. The first check runs a minute after goui starts, then twice a day. A release that changes only the panel applies on its own. A release that changes the engine waits for Update to … in the menu. That update restarts goui. macOS then asks again to let goui record audio. Check for updates… offers the same install in a dialog, with Update and Later. goui checks GitHub for new versions and sends nothing about you.

goui starts at login and restarts after a crash. If the sound stops, it rebuilds the audio path on its own. After an install or an update, goui opens its panel once, so the menu bar item is easy to find.

The wide panel

Pick Wide panel in the menu. Listen, Tune and Library stand side by side, as they do in a wide browser side panel. A second pick takes the panel back to its narrow width. You can also drag an edge of the panel to the width you want. The height follows the page.

Terminal

One line installs goui, or updates it. It needs no admin password. It checks the Mac, says what to expect and asks before it installs.

curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/wyndhamharyata/goui/main/mac/install.sh | sh

The installer puts a goui command in Terminal.

goui set "name"Plays a Set, the same as a pick from the menu. It exits with 0 once the Set plays
goui start, goui stop, goui restartStart, stop or restart the app
goui logsFollows the log in ~/Library/Logs/goui.log
goui rollbackPuts back the app from before the last update
goui uninstallRemoves the app. Settings stay in ~/Library/Application Support/goui

Measure your ear

An AutoEQ profile tunes the headphone to sound neutral, not your ear. EQByEar measures what your ear still wants on top of it.

1 Turn My ear offIn the Library, open My ear and press Measure my ear. My ear turns off so the test hears the headphone alone.
2 Run the testeqbyear.com opens in your browser. goui already hears the browser, so run the test right away.
3 Paste the resultCopy the result and come back to goui. Press Paste in My ear, paste it in the box, then press Apply.
4 Save itPress Save to keep it as an ear preset. Turn My ear on to hear the headphone with your ear.

Troubleshooting

! AirPods and Beats sound flat or lose head trackingmacOS applies Spatial Audio and head tracking only when an app plays straight to AirPods or Beats. goui plays in between, so the effect stops or can sound wrong. Turn off Spatial Audio for the headphone in Control Center, or quit goui while you use them.
! No sound, or the sound plays without the EQCheck that goui is allowed in System Settings, under Privacy & Security, then Screen & System Audio Recording. Then pick Restart engine in the menu. The panel's status line shows when the engine waits to try again, with its error.
! The sound is louder or quieter than it wasAnother system EQ, such as eqMac or SoundSource, stacks its curve on goui's. Quit one of them.
! goui set says goui is not runningRun goui start, then try again. If the panel has not loaded yet, pick Reload panel in the menu.
! An update went wronggoui rollback puts back the app from before the last update. goui logs shows what happened.

Privacy

  • goui reads the Mac's audio, runs it through the EQ and plays it back. It never records, stores or sends it.
  • Settings, presets and Sets stay in ~/Library/Application Support/goui.
  • AutoEQ downloads work as in the extension. The privacy policy has the details.
  • goui checks GitHub for new releases twice a day. That request carries nothing about you.
